# MATII TECHNOLOGIES content strategy

This strategy maps to the existing four pillars and current routes. These are research hypotheses, **not measured keyword volumes or guaranteed opportunities**. Validate terminology using sales inquiries, Search Console, customer interviews and a chosen keyword research tool before commissioning major copy changes.

## Brand and business searches

Use MATII TECHNOLOGIES consistently in titles, company profiles, author biographies and owned descriptions. The confirmed website is `matiitechnologies.com`. Do not rename email or booking accounts by guessing. Track brand queries, domain queries and actual misspellings found in Search Console separately. A legacy name can be explained on a genuine company-history page if the owner approves; do not create artificial pages to capture variations.

The homepage introduces enterprise software, AI/data, BPO and managed cloud services. `/about` should establish who performs the work and why buyers can trust the team. `/contact` is the conversion destination. Proposed additions such as leadership biographies, client permissions and business registration details require real evidence and owner review.

## Service intent mapping

### `/services/enterprise`

Primary intent: evaluate a partner for enterprise software and core-system development. Research custom software development, enterprise application modernization, ERP implementation/integration, fintech infrastructure, secure API integration, and web/mobile application engineering where the actual delivery team offers them.

Recommended content additions: a clear scope boundary, examples of supported integrations, migration approach, acceptance criteria and operational handover. Link to real project records with the exact relevant technology/problem. Do not claim regulated compliance, banking certifications or a delivery timeline without evidence.

### `/services/bpo`

Primary intent: evaluate an operations partner. Research customer support outsourcing, back-office operations, data operations, dedicated delivery teams, and business-process automation. Explain what is handled by people versus software, supported languages/hours, training, access controls and escalation ownership. These details need operational confirmation.

Link from enterprise-system articles when an implementation requires ongoing operations, and from contact pages when a buyer needs a dedicated team. Avoid conflating all BPO work with AI automation.

### `/services/ai`

Primary intent: evaluate the MATII Data & AI Platform and associated services. Its visible product portfolio includes MATII Connect, Lakehouse, Catalog, Analytics, Knowledge, ML, Models, Agents and Studio. Product cards and anchors live within the service page; there are no independent `/products/[slug]` routes.

Research data integration and pipelines, enterprise knowledge search/RAG, data governance, analytics, model deployment and workflow agents. Propose practical explanations of source-system connectivity, permissions, evaluation, deployment boundaries and human review. Make a product detail page only after real documentation, positioning and unique content exist. Until then, link to actual product anchors from relevant articles; do not manufacture sitemap URLs for them.

### `/services/cloud`

Primary intent: evaluate managed cloud and reliability services. Research cloud migration, DevOps delivery pipelines, SRE monitoring, infrastructure modernization, security operations and cost visibility. Proposed additions: responsibility matrix, onboarding steps, supported platforms, recovery objectives and how reliability is measured. No invented SLA, uptime, certifications or customer scale.

## Ethiopia and localization

The site visibly identifies Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. This supports honest local context such as software development in Ethiopia and Addis Ababa engineering teams where relevant. Explain actual delivery/communication arrangements and local buyer needs. Do not repeat city names across every heading or create duplicate city pages. Overseas locations shown in existing content need owner verification before local business listings or address schema; the office-address configuration currently contains no verified street addresses.

The configured locales are English, Afaan Oromo (`om`), Amharic (`am`), German (`de`) and Polish (`pl`). English is unprefixed. Preserve the existing translated dictionaries and have fluent reviewers assess accuracy. CMS content is not automatically a translated edition because its surrounding navigation is translated. Publish substantive translations before expecting additional hreflang entries. News index, internships and talent-pool body copy is currently English; their canonical language remains English.

## Evidence-driven case studies

Use `/projects` and the existing project detail route. The repository includes example-looking records and large outcome claims; the SEO work does not authenticate them. Before promotion, get client approval, verify public names, and retain the measurement method behind every number.

Recommended structure: client context where authorized; business problem; constraints; what MATII delivered; architecture and tradeoffs; implementation/release process; outcome with baseline, period and units; lessons; a relevant service link and contact CTA. Anonymous case studies are acceptable if the work and outcomes are real. Do not imply a client is a sponsor just because its name appears on a project.

## Editorial opportunities

Potential articles for engineers to author using real experience:

- Enterprise integration: how to plan an ERP/API migration while keeping operations running.
- Data platforms: a worked example connecting source data to governed analytics using the actual MATII platform.
- AI: how the team evaluates retrieval quality and handles access permissions in enterprise knowledge search.
- Cloud: a real migration checklist, including rollback, observability and handover.
- Operations: how a BPO team documents escalation and quality checks across a client workflow.
- Product engineering: lessons from a permission-cleared web or mobile delivery, with concrete decisions and outcomes.

Avoid publishing generic summaries solely to fill a calendar. Add original examples, diagrams, code or measurements that a qualified author can stand behind. Label opinions, experiments and product capabilities accurately. Never turn draft/sample CMS records into purported company announcements.

## Internal links and conversion paths

Use descriptive link text: “enterprise integration services,” “managed cloud operations,” or the actual case-study title. Link each real project to its relevant pillar and each pillar to relevant evidence. Link technical articles to the product/service they demonstrate and to related articles when useful. Keep breadcrumbs and locale-aware links consistent. Resolve the broken-link audit before adding more navigation entries; removing a CMS project must also trigger review of hand-authored menu links.

Blog filtering is client-side, so there are no indexable search-query pages to promote. Service aliases canonicalize to their existing pillar URLs. Share canonical links, not alternate alias URLs.

## Measurement and editorial approval

Assign one owner per pillar to confirm services, examples and claims. Before publishing, check a unique title, accurate description, real primary heading, useful links, image rights/alt text, and mobile readability. The CMS snippet preview is an approximation; search engines may choose different titles/descriptions. Editorial keywords are planning aids, not a ranking score.

Review qualified inquiries and project fit alongside organic clicks. Separate job applications from sales leads. Compare performance by pillar, locale, device and branded/non-branded query groups. Use the 7/30/60/90-day plan in [SEO-SETUP-AND-GROWTH-GUIDE.md](SEO-SETUP-AND-GROWTH-GUIDE.md). Major visible copy changes remain proposals for company approval; this implementation changes metadata and brand errors, not service promises.
